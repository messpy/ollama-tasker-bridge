AI Model Bridge runs language and vision models on Android and connects them to Tasker and MacroDroid.

Features:

* Run supported local models on the device.
* Connect to an Ollama server when remote inference is preferred.
* Download and manage models from supported sources.
* Use prompts, system prompts, and generation settings in the test chat.
* Trigger inference from Tasker or MacroDroid.

Local inference is available without an Ollama account. Network services are optional.
